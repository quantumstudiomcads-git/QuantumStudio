not a promise that every item is executable.

Wannierization has a source-defined workflow; broader Wannier/Berry work is
mostly planned. NEGF/device-transport items are public discoverability, but the
current adapter is not public Calculate routing. Molecular-dynamics,
thermodynamic, defect, topological, and advanced post-processing entries must be
read through their status label and planned reference.
