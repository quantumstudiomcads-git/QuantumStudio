# Atomic Charge Transfer (Bader)

**Current status:** `READY`

Quantum Studio defines pseudopotential-referenced net atomic charge as

$$
q_i
=
Z_{\mathrm{val},i}
-
N_{\mathrm{Bader},i}.
$$

- Positive $q_i$ means electron deficiency.
- Negative $q_i$ means electron accumulation.

Atomic Charge Transfer is a derived result from the same Bader result; it does not rerun Bader.
