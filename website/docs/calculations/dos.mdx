# Density of States

**Current status:** `READY`

The total DOS is

$$
D(E)
=
\sum_{n\mathbf k}
w_{\mathbf k}
\delta(E-\epsilon_{n\mathbf k}).
$$

Quantum Studio automatically constructs the required private electronic stages and returns a terminal `DOSResult`.

Analyze provides the total DOS, Fermi marker, plotting, filtering where relevant, and export.
