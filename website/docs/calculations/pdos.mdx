# Projected Density of States

**Current status:** `READY`

A projected DOS is

$$
D_\alpha(E)
=
\sum_{n\mathbf k}
w_{\mathbf k}
|\langle\phi_\alpha|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle|^2
\delta(E-\epsilon_{n\mathbf k}).
$$

For collinear spin, separate up/down channels are meaningful.

For SOC, Quantum Studio must not invent conventional spin-up/down channels from a noncollinear spinor calculation. See [SOC PDOS](./soc-pdos).
