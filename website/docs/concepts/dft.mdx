# Density Functional Theory

Quantum Studio exposes DFT workflows without attempting to hide the approximations that define them.

A Kohn–Sham calculation solves a self-consistent one-electron problem of the form

$$
\left[-\frac{1}{2}\nabla^2 + V_{\mathrm{eff}}[n](\mathbf r)\right]\psi_i(\mathbf r)
= \epsilon_i\psi_i(\mathbf r),
$$

with the density reconstructed from the occupied states.

Future concept pages will connect each GUI parameter to the physical and numerical assumption it controls.
